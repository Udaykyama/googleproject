Two tools for problems Google is actively fixing

InboxReady

Since November 2025 Gmail rejects mail from bulk senders that fail its authentication requirements — at the SMTP layer, so the message never reaches the mailbox. InboxReady checks SPF, DKIM, DMARC, reverse DNS, transport security and one-click unsubscribe, and reports what Gmail would do about each.

Every finding carries a severity, the reason, a concrete fix, and the standard it derives from.

Review moderation

Google reported removing over 240 million fake or policy-violating reviews in 2024. This scores a batch for the signals that betray a review farm — templated phrasing, near-duplicate text, rating extremes, brand-new accounts, same-day bursts — and routes anything uncertain to a human rather than deleting it.

Nothing here proves a review is fake. The output is a work queue.
